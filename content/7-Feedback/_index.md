## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

Throughout the internship, I had the opportunity to work in a professional, collaborative, and supportive environment. Team members were always willing to share their knowledge and provide guidance whenever I encountered technical challenges. The working environment also encouraged independent learning and continuous improvement, allowing interns to develop both technical and problem-solving skills.

### 2. Mentor Support

My mentor closely monitored the project's progress and provided valuable technical guidance throughout the internship. Rather than giving direct answers, my mentor encouraged me to research documentation, explore different approaches, and analyze problems independently before discussing possible solutions. This mentoring approach significantly improved my self-learning ability and strengthened my understanding of AWS cloud services and serverless architecture.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Background

The assigned tasks were closely aligned with my major in Information Technology and my interest in Cloud Computing. The internship project allowed me to apply knowledge gained from university courses, including programming, database systems, computer networks, and software architecture, while also exposing me to real-world cloud technologies and development practices.

### 4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development

This internship provided valuable hands-on experience with a wide range of AWS services, including Amazon S3, Amazon API Gateway, AWS Lambda, Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon EventBridge, Amazon SQS, EventBridge Pipes, AWS Step Functions, AWS Elemental MediaConvert, Amazon CloudFront, and Amazon CloudWatch.

In addition to technical knowledge, I improved essential professional skills such as technical documentation, troubleshooting, system analysis, independent research, and project implementation following a structured development process.

### 5. Working Culture and Team Collaboration

The working culture emphasized collaboration, knowledge sharing, and mutual support among team members. Everyone was approachable and willing to provide constructive feedback whenever necessary. This positive environment encouraged continuous learning and motivated me to become more confident when solving technical problems independently.

### 6. Internship Program

The internship program was well organized with a clear learning roadmap that gradually guided interns from fundamental AWS concepts to implementing a complete cloud-native application. Learning materials, technical documentation, and practical exercises were carefully prepared, making it easier for interns to understand cloud technologies and apply them effectively throughout the project.

## Additional Questions

### What was the most satisfying part of your internship?

The most rewarding experience was having the opportunity to design and implement a complete **Serverless Video-on-Demand Platform on AWS**. Throughout the project, I participated in every development stage, including architecture design, AWS infrastructure deployment, backend development, implementation of the video processing pipeline, web application integration, system testing, and performance optimization.

Working on a real-world cloud application significantly enhanced my understanding of modern serverless architecture and provided practical experience that extends beyond classroom learning.

### What aspects of the program could be improved?

One possible improvement would be to organize additional technical sharing sessions covering real-world cloud architectures, enterprise deployment strategies, and industry best practices. These sessions would provide interns with broader perspectives on how cloud-native applications are designed and operated in production environments.

### Would you recommend this internship program to others?

Yes. I would highly recommend this internship program to students who are interested in Cloud Computing and AWS technologies. The program offers a structured learning path, extensive hands-on experience, meaningful project work, and continuous guidance from experienced mentors, making it an excellent opportunity for both technical and professional development.

## Suggestions and Future Expectations

For future internship programs, I hope additional project topics could be introduced in areas such as DevOps, Machine Learning, Data Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Cloud Security. This would allow interns to explore different technology domains based on their individual interests and career goals.

I also believe that organizing more technical workshops or seminars led by experienced cloud engineers would provide valuable insights into current industry trends, enterprise-scale architectures, and practical software engineering methodologies.

Overall, this internship has been an invaluable learning experience that strengthened both my technical expertise and professional skills. If given the opportunity, I would be delighted to participate in future advanced training programs or cloud-related projects to continue expanding my knowledge and experience.

Finally, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to my mentor and everyone involved in the internship program for their continuous support, guidance, and encouragement throughout this meaningful learning journey.
